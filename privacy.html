Snoremap

Privacy Policy

Effective 28 September 2026. This policy covers the Snoremap apps for iPhone and Android, published by DIRWAAS INTELLIGENT SOLUTIONS LLC, Amman, Jordan.

What is recorded

While a night is recording, Snoremap listens through the microphone and saves the audio to your phone in short files.

What is kept

When the night ends, the recording is analyzed on your phone. Short clips of detected snoring and a line of loudness levels are kept. The rest of the audio is deleted.

What leaves your phone

Nothing you record. Snoremap has no account and no server. Audio, scores and tags are never uploaded to us or anyone else. Recordings and nights are also kept out of cloud backups. We do not use analytics, advertising or tracking of any kind.

Purchases

Subscriptions are handled by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never see your payment details. Those companies process purchases under their own privacy policies.

Sharing

A clip or PDF leaves your phone only when you share it yourself, to a destination you choose.

Other people

Anyone sleeping near you may be recorded too. Please let them know.

Deleting your data

Delete a single night from its report, or everything from Settings. Deleting the app deletes all its data.

Children

Snoremap is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal data from anyone.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will appear on this page with a new date. Questions: contact@dirwaas.com.
