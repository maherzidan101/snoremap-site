Common questions

Does it work with the screen locked? Yes. Start a night, lock your phone and leave it charging near the bed. In the morning open Snoremap and stop the night.

Why is a clip not snoring? Fans, pets or another sleeper can sound like snoring. Mark the clip "Not snoring" or "Someone else" and its minutes leave your score.

How do I cancel Snoremap Plus? On iPhone: Settings, your name, Subscriptions. On Android: Google Play, Payments and subscriptions, Subscriptions. Your nights stay in the app after you cancel.

Restore a purchase. Open Settings in Snoremap and tap Restore.

Snoremap is not a medical device. If you are worried about your breathing during sleep, talk to a doctor.
